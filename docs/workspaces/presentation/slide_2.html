Workspace A screens for suitability before design begins

[1] Uses a common intake card for purpose, inputs, impact, ownership, metrics, and rollback.
[2] Scores seven dimensions: value, input stability, data minimization, containment, oversight, testability, observability.
[3] Assigns risk tiers R0–R3 to determine the permitted action boundary.
[4] Produces a hold, draft-only, human-reviewed prototype, controlled-test, or restricted-use recommendation.
